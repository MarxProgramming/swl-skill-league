How scoring works

Base skills and leaps: 10 points. Upgrades: 5. Pro upgrades: 2.5. A perfect mark earns double, replacing the achieved score.

Strong basics earn the most; advanced skills add smaller gains. Skills score independently. Upgrades do not tick earlier skills, and each completed three-skill group earns a bonus: +5 when all three are Achieved or better, rising to +10 when all three are Perfect. The +10 replaces the +5; they do not stack. This includes the three leaps. Linking and presentation earn a separate bonus in each Rolls and Acro complex: Good +1, Excellent +2, Perfect +3. Choose one rating; these bonuses do not double. Bonuses follow the current marks, so resetting a skill can remove a completion bonus. All three perfect skills also unlock a celebration. The ring shows your share of the available points. Achieved totals include perfect skills.

How the league works

  • The top five are on show. Each board has a blurred glimpse of sixth place. Press a visible name to open their skills.
  • Good foundations earn more. Base skills and leaps earn 10 points, upgrades 5, and pro upgrades 2.5.Harder skills add smaller gains. This rewards strong basics and gives gymnasts building their foundations a chance to keep up with more advanced gymnasts.
  • Perfect earns double. It replaces the Achieved score. Achieved means the skill would count without a downgrade, with no more than 0.5 deductions; Perfect allows no more than 0.2.
  • Complete the group for a bonus. All three skills Achieved or better earn +5. All three Perfect earn +10 instead. This includes leaps. The bonus follows the current marks.
  • Controlled linking and presentation earn extra. In each Rolls or Acro complex, choose Good +1, Excellent +2, or Perfect +3. Judge smooth transitions, balance and presentation. Leaps have no linking rating.
  • Reveal your place. On a gymnast’s card, choose Reveal placement to see two people above and below on each board, with blurred previews of the third. Equal scores are ordered alphabetically.
